For a better experience while using our Application, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information.

Our app utilizes Supabase as a backend service to deliver prompt categories and text prompts to your device. ### 2. Advertising and Analytics

Our Application uses third-party advertising networks, specifically ironSource LevelPlay (Unity), to display advertisements. To serve personalized ads, analyze ad performance, and prevent fraud, these third-party SDKs may collect and transmit certain data, including:
- **Advertising ID / Device IDs**: A unique, user-resettable ID for advertising (such as the Android Advertising ID).
- **Installed Applications / Apps on Device**: Information regarding the applications currently installed on your device. This is used by our advertising partners for ad targeting, campaign attribution, and fraud prevention. Furthermore, our Application natively queries for specific installed apps (such as ChatGPT and Gemini) to enable the seamless launching of these tools directly from our app.
- **Usage Data**: Information about your interactions with the app and advertisements.
- **Device Information**: Information about your device, OS, and network.

This data is processed and shared with our advertising partners for marketing, advertising, and analytical purposes. You can opt-out of personalized advertising through your device's privacy or Google settings. Please review the privacy policy of our partner (ironSource / Unity) for more detailed information.
